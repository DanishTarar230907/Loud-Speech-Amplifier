Core Technology

Built for Loud-Listener Patients

Every feature is engineered for clinical reliability. Pure mathematical DSP with zero AI guessing — behaving exactly like a $4,000 medical hearing aid.

⚡

100× Bionic Amplification

A Wide Dynamic Range Compressor (WDRC) intelligently tracks the volume of every 20ms audio frame and applies up to 100× amplification to faint voices from across the room.

🔇

Surgical Noise Gate

A mathematical Downward Expander measures room silence and cleanly mutes background hiss and air conditioning. The instant a voice appears, the gate opens with zero latency.

🧠

Cloud AI Transcription

Powered by Google's Cloud Speech-to-Text AI configured for Hindi/Urdu. Every spoken word is accurately transcribed in Roman Urdu characters in real-time on the screen.

🛡️

Soft-Clip Ear Protection

A tanh()-based mathematical limiter ensures loud sudden sounds (like a door slamming) are instantly compressed so they never reach a painful volume in the patient's ears.

Pipeline Architecture

5 Steps From Whisper to Clear

The entire pipeline completes within a single 20ms frame window for imperceptible real-time latency.

01

Microphone Capture

Web Audio API streams live audio from the microphone at 48,000 Hz, split into continuous 20ms processing frames.

02

Noise Gate

A Downward Expander measures volume. Silent frames (fan noise, air hiss) are immediately muted. The gate opens the instant any voice is detected.

03

WDRC Amplifier

A slow-release envelope tracker applies massive amplification to every whisper and distant voice, maintaining consistent loud output.

04

Soft Clip & Output

A tanh() Soft Clipper limits the output to a safe level. The clean, amplified audio is immediately sent to the headphone output.

05

Live Transcription

Google's Web Speech API simultaneously transcribes everything in Roman Urdu and English, printing each sentence live on the screen.
